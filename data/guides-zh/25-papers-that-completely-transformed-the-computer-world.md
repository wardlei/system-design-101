![彻底改变计算机世界的 25 篇论文](https://assets.bytebytego.com/diagrams/0419-25-papers-that-completely-transformed-the-computer-world.png)

以下是 25 篇对计算机科学领域产生重大影响的论文：

*   [Dynamo - Amazon’s Highly Available Key Value Store](https://www.allthingsdistributed.com/files/amazon-dynamo-sosp2007.pdf)

*   [Google File System](https://static.googleusercontent.com/media/research.google.com/en//archive/gfs-sosp2003.pdf)：深入了解一个高可扩展的文件系统

*   [Scaling Memcached at Facebook](https://research.facebook.com/file/839620310074473/scaling-memcache-at-facebook.pdf)：审视缓存的复杂性

*   [BigTable](https://static.googleusercontent.com/media/research.google.com/en//archive/bigtable-osdi06.pdf)：分布式存储系统背后的设计原则

*   [Borg - Large Scale Cluster Management at Google](https://storage.googleapis.com/pub-tools-public-publication-data/pdf/43438.pdf)

*   [Cassandra](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/projects/ladis2009/papers/lakshman-ladis2009.pdf)：了解分布式 NoSQL 数据库的设计与架构

*   [Attention Is All You Need](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762)：走进名为 Transformer 的全新深度学习架构

*   [Kafka](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2017/09/Kafka.pdf)：分布式消息平台的内部机制

*   [FoundationDB](https://www.foundationdb.org/files/fdb-paper.pdf)：了解分布式数据库的运作方式

*   [Amazon Aurora](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs245/readings/aurora.pdf)：了解 Amazon 如何实现高可用性与高性能

*   [Spanner](https://static.googleusercontent.com/media/research.google.com/en//archive/spanner-osdi2012.pdf)：Google 全球分布式数据库的设计与架构

*   [MapReduce](https://storage.googleapis.com/pub-tools-public-publication-data/pdf/16cb30b4b92fd4989b8619a61752a2387c6dd474.pdf)：详细了解 MapReduce 如何实现海量数据的并行处理

*   [Shard Manager](https://dl.acm.org/doi/pdf/10.1145/3477132.3483546)：理解通用的分片管理框架

*   [Dapper](https://static.googleusercontent.com/media/research.google.com/en//archive/papers/dapper-2010-1.pdf)：深入了解 Google 的分布式系统追踪基础设施

*   [Flink](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/308993790_Apache_Flink_Stream_and_Batch_Processing_in_a_Single_Engine)：详细了解流处理与批处理的统一架构

*   [A Comprehensive Survey on Vector Databases](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.11703.pdf)

*   [Zanzibar](https://storage.googleapis.com/pub-tools-public-publication-data/pdf/10683a8987dbf0c6d4edcafb9b4f05cc9de5974a.pdf)：了解 Google 全球访问控制列表管理系统的设计、实现与部署

*   [Monarch](https://storage.googleapis.com/pub-tools-public-publication-data/pdf/d84ab6c93881af998de877d0070a706de7bec6d8.pdf)：Google 内存时间序列数据库的架构

*   [Thrift](https://thrift.apache.org/static/files/thrift-20070401.pdf)：探索 Facebook 代码生成工具背后的设计选择

*   [Bitcoin](https://bitcoin.org/bitcoin.pdf)：点对点电子现金系统的开创性介绍

*   [WTF - Who to Follow Service at Twitter](https://web.stanford.edu/~rezab/papers/wtf_overview.pdf)：Twitter（现为 X）的用户推荐系统

*   [MyRocks: LSM-Tree Database Storage Engine](https://www.vldb.org/pvldb/vol13/p3217-matsunobu.pdf)

*   [GoTo Considered Harmful](https://homepages.cwi.nl/~storm/teaching/reader/Dijkstra68.pdf)

*   [Raft Consensus Algorithm](https://raft.github.io/raft.pdf)：了解更易理解的共识算法

*   [Time Clocks and Ordering of Events](https://lamport.azurewebsites.net/pubs/time-clocks.pdf)：极其重要的论文，解释了分布式系统中时间与事件排序的概念
